Roman Empire:

The Roman Empire was one of the most powerful and influential civilizations in history. Originating in Rome, it expanded across Europe, North Africa, and parts of Asia, lasting from 27 BCE to 476 CE in the West and until 1453 CE in the East (Byzantine Empire). Known for its advanced engineering, strong military, and monumental architecture such as the Colosseum and aqueducts, Rome laid the foundations for modern law, governance, and culture.
